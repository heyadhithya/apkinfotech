# Design System: APK Infotech

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Enterprise training catalogue"**

The complete enterprise training site keeps APK’s intact official logo and navy-and-gold identity across a shared white header, navy photographic mastheads, programme pages, and readable white content. The home hero uses two direct actions; programme discovery, archive viewing, and course-aware enquiry each have their own clear place. Self-hosted Manrope provides the consistent typographic voice.

This was a code-led refinement preserving the committed, user-pinned brand and enterprise world. A new concept roll and seed were omitted; no approved visual comp or quality-bar card was available. AWS Training and IBM reference access was blocked, so visual fidelity remains unverified. The direction and scope are recorded in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-complete-site.md`; no seed or visual approval is implied.

The user’s AWS Training and IBM references establish the corporate direction; this remains an original interpretation without verified live-reference fidelity. Official programme topics, registration, and contact information are recorded in `docs/official-site-sources.json`. Documentary photographs remain explicitly labeled past activities. Claims about current fees, batches, eligibility, certification, trainers, and outcomes require confirmation.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Official navy/gold identity with a white header and navy masthead.
- Readable Manrope with restrained heading weights.
- Three-column photographic programme catalogue and native programme discovery.
- Flat surfaces, native disclosures, and bounded motion.

## Colors

Official navy and gold pair with white reading surfaces and a cool-neutral archive and pale enquiry/detail panels. Frontmatter contains the exact source colors; local neutrals support photographs, borders, empty results, and the dark contact section.

- **Navy** (`navy`): headings, selected category controls, and contact background.
- **Action Blue** (`blue`): primary actions, links, and registration rows; primary hover deepens to navy.
- **Gold** (`gold`): course categories and focus outlines. The lighter `finder-gold` fills the masthead action and enquiry link.
- **Paper** (`paper`): white page and sticky header, matching programme cards and fields.
- **Muted** (`muted`): supporting copy and archive captions. Fine cool dividers organize white content; the same cool dividers organize the archive.

**The Evidence Rule.** Archive images describe past APK activity; they do not verify current course sessions or the current office.

## Typography

Manrope is self-hosted with WOFF2 weights 400, 500, 600, 700, and 800 and `font-display: swap`; Arial and sans-serif are fallbacks. Frontmatter records actual roles and responsive sizes rather than an invented mathematical scale.

- Hero: `display`, weight 600; 46px at ≤1100px, 44px at ≤760px, and `clamp(33px, 9.5vw, 38px)` at ≤480px. Supporting copy is 17px/1.7, becoming 16px on mobile; maximum width is 520px.
- Section heading: 36px/700, becoming 30px at ≤760px. Contact heading is 42px/600, becoming 36px on mobile.
- Programme heading: 21px/700, becoming 22px at ≤760px, 20px at ≤680px, and 19px at ≤480px. Archive heading: 20px/700, becoming 19px on mobile.
- Body: 16px/400 with 1.65 line height. Programme summaries use 14px and FAQ questions use 15px; disclosure copy and actions use 14px; filter/status/category labels use 13px; photograph captions use 12px.
- Official wordmark: 18px/800 with a 9px/600 tracked caption; at ≤760px it uses 16px and 8px; at ≤480px it uses 15px and 7px. Preserve these logo-specific roles separately from reading text.

## Layout

The main container is `min(1200px, calc(100% - 112px))`; at ≤1100px its width is `calc(100% - 64px)` and at ≤760px `calc(100% - 48px)`. Section spacing is 64px vertically, becoming 44px on mobile. At ≤480px the container is `calc(100% - 36px)`. The sticky header is 88px tall, becoming 76px on mobile; anchor offsets are 105px and 90px respectively.

Hero columns are `1.18fr 1fr` with a 64px gap, reduced to 36px at ≤1100px. At ≤760px they stack with a 30px gap. Hero image ratios are 1.35 desktop, 1.8 mobile, and 1.55 at ≤480px. The hero uses wrapping gold Explore courses and white-outline Enquire now actions. The finder sits in a course-section native disclosure, with select/action columns and one column at ≤480px.

Three featured programmes appear initially in three columns with a 24px gap; expanding reveals all seven. at ≤1100px the grid becomes two columns. At ≤760px the gap becomes 20px; at ≤680px the directory becomes one column of compact thumbnail cards: 96px images, 16px padding/gap; at ≤480px images are 80px and padding/gap are 14px/12px. Programme thumbnail images are square and their caption uses 10px text. Programme photos use a 2.4 aspect ratio. The archive uses four columns with a 22px gap, two at ≤1100px, and one at ≤480px. Archive images use a 1.45 ratio; at ≤480px each archive entry becomes a 96px-thumbnail row with square photography. Archive title/summary minimum heights are removed at ≤1100px.

Filter buttons wrap. Search is 285px wide and moves to full width at ≤1100px. FAQ columns are `0.8fr 1.2fr` with a 70px gap, stacking at ≤760px. Contact columns are `0.9fr 1.1fr` with a 96px gap (54px at ≤1100px), stacking at ≤760px. Phone/email columns are two on desktop, one at ≤1100px, two at ≤760px, and one at ≤480px. Footer groups stack on mobile. About uses three columns (40px gap), stacking at ≤760px; internships use two columns (64px gap), stacking at ≤760px. Detail content uses `1.55fr 1fr` with a 64px gap, then a single column at ≤760px. Related programme cards become one column on mobile. Enquiry fields use two columns (20px 16px gap) and a full-row message, stacking at ≤760px. Legal pages cap reading width at 800px.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat: no box shadows are implemented. Depth comes from the navy masthead/contact surfaces, cool archive/enquiry layers, white programme cards, fine borders, framed photographs, and a solid navy contact section.

One hero frame reveal uses `archive-frame` for 600ms, clipping from `inset(0 7% 0 0)` to `inset(0)`; its image settles from scale 1.035 to 1 over 850ms. Both use `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. Fine-pointer hover scales programme images to 1.025 over 240ms. Arrow and disclosure transforms use 180ms with that easing; interactive colors/borders use 160ms with default easing. Reduced-motion disables transitions, animations, spatial hover transforms, and smooth scrolling while retaining content and disclosure states.

## Shapes

Buttons, native selects, and the hero frame use 2px corners. Filter/search controls, programme containers, and inner programme/archive photo frames use 4px corners. The official logo uses 3px and archive badges 2px. Programme photographs are inset 12px inside cards. Borders remain thin; no pills or ornamental subject tiles are implemented.

## Components

### Buttons and links

Actions use 14px/600 labels, 12px 20px padding, 48px minimum height, 14px icon gap, and 2px corners. Primary is action-blue/white; outline is transparent with blue text/border and cool hover; white is white/navy with warm hover. The finder action uses light gold/navy and a lighter gold hover. Text links are blue and underline on hover. Button/link arrows translate 3px on hover or keyboard focus. Global focus is a 3px gold outline with 5px offset.

### Programme finder and enquiry

The course-section Have a programme in mind? disclosure contains a labeled native select with All programmes and the seven verified titles. View programme resets category, expands results, and matches the selected full title; All programmes clears query. Submission focuses the programme heading and scrolls it into view. Changing the selection alone does not submit.

The home enquiry and `/enquire` page share a course-aware form. Name/email and explicit consent are required; phone/message are optional. Programme choices include Help me choose, Internships, and verified titles; a valid course query preselects the choice. Labels, autocomplete, associated field errors, an alert summary, and first-invalid-field focus support completion. Inputs/textarea have 3px corners and 48px minimum height; selects use 16px text in the full form. The consent checkbox is 20px with a blue accent. Error text/borders are red, alert panels pale red; pending/success actions are disabled with 0.65 opacity. Editing clears stale draft/status, and retry keeps entered details.

Without a configured lead service, submission validates locally and displays a reviewable, unsent WhatsApp draft. A separate explicit action opens WhatsApp; no submission or storage is claimed. The official Google Form remains an alternative. When a valid configured HTTPS lead endpoint is present, server acceptance is required before a success message; error/rate-limit states offer retry and draft fallback. The implementation has no local enquiry database or PII logs; external retention is unconfirmed. Anonymous conversion events stay in the browser. No live lead credential was supplied. Native focus, privacy links, and no-JavaScript direct-contact guidance remain visible.

### Filters and search

Native category buttons use `aria-pressed` in an accessible group and reference their results container. They have 44px minimum height and 13px text; selected controls are navy/white, while unselected ones are transparent/muted with a thin border. Hover changes text/border to navy.

Native labeled search fields have white fill, 4px corners, 44px minimum height, and a 13px input. The containing field gets a 2px gold focus outline with 3px offset. Case-insensitive trimmed query and category filters intersect. Course search matches title, category, description, and topics; archive search matches title, tags, and summary. Result counts are announced.

### Programme directory

Photographic white cards use a cool 1px border and 4px outer corners. Body padding is 16px 20px 20px at all sizes. Desktop programme titles/summaries use 55px/69px minimum heights, removed at ≤760px. An archive caption sits below every photo; gold category text follows the programme heading. Category metadata sits below the card heading with an 8px top margin. There are no subject icons or topic chips.

Three featured programmes appear initially. The expand button toggles all seven with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`. Active query/category filtering shows all matches regardless of featured state. Clear course filters restores the three featured cards; the empty-state Show all courses action clears filters and expands all seven. Native Programme overview disclosure rotates its chevron 180 degrees. Course details navigates to the canonical programme page; Enquire about this course opens `/enquire?course=<id>` with a preselected programme.

Programme photos default to `object-position: center 46%`. Workshop-presentation, classroom-discussion, and internship-classroom frames use `center top` to keep embedded GPS labels outside the visible crop. Preserve those frame-specific crops and original photo provenance; do not treat them as images of the named current course.

### Activity archive

Archive cards remain unboxed on the cool-neutral archive section. A paper badge labels each image Past activity. The title leads, followed by muted activity tags with an 8px top margin, summary, and native View activity disclosure. The disclosure arrow rotates 90 degrees when open. Empty results use a warm flat panel with a reset action. The archive is the activity collection. View photo opens a native modal dialog with a navy surface, translucent navy backdrop, contained full photo, and past-activity caption. The Close photo action and Escape close the dialog and restore focus to the launch button. The dialog is capped at `min(1000px, 95vw)` and 92dvh.

### Navigation and official identity

Header/footer retain the full official logo image and separate wordmark. The image is contained at 65px × 52px, becoming 55px × 44px at ≤760px and 48px × 40px at ≤480px. Shared navigation covers Home, Courses, Internships, Activities, About, and Contact plus Enquire now. Home-section links work from other routes; course pages mark Courses active and the enquiry page marks its action current. Active hash/route links use blue and a 2px underline. Internal links remain in the same tab. At ≤1100px a 44px menu button toggles a vertical paper panel directly below the sticky header, with `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`; opening focuses the first link, Escape closes it and restores toggle focus, and link selection, outside pointer activity, or focus leaving the header closes it. The dropdown scrolls within the available viewport. Preserve the keyboard-visible skip link. The footer provides official contact actions and Privacy/Website terms links on every page.

### Programme detail and supporting content

Seven statically generated programme pages use breadcrumbs, a navy masthead, real archive photo, enquiry/official registration actions, confirmed topic tags, readable overview/practical-learning sections, an information-to-confirm aside, native FAQ, and two related programmes. Topic chips use 3px corners, 8px 14px padding, and pale fill/border; the aside uses 28px padding. Course titles use `clamp(36px, 3.4vw, 48px)`, then 38px/34px at the mobile/narrow breakpoints. Generic enquiry/legal/state titles use `clamp(34px, 4vw, 52px)`. About uses verified scope/location facts and a Contact our team action; internships distinguish photographed past work from opportunities that must be confirmed.

### FAQ and contact

FAQ uses native details with browser disclosure markers, thin dividers, 20px summary padding, and 14px answer text. Contact consolidates the official office, map link, phones, email, and WhatsApp on navy; supporting text is pale, links are gold-tinted, and its action is white. Call-ahead guidance remains visible. External links use `target="_blank"` and `rel="noreferrer"`; phone and email actions use native URLs.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** preserve the full official logo without redrawing, recoloring, or cropping it.
- **Do** label archive photography as past activity and preserve meaningful alt text.
- **Do** use navy actions, gold discovery emphasis, white reading space, and the observed readable type roles.
- **Do** retain keyboard focus, native disclosure, filter announcements, and reduced-motion behavior.

### Don't:
- **Don't** imply photographs show a specific current course session or the current office.
- **Don't** invent fees, batches, durations, placement guarantees, ratings, or accreditation.
- **Don't** restore decorative subject icons, pastel course panels, or duplicate story/gallery sections.
- **Don't** introduce looping motion, repeated scroll reveals, or card hover shadows.
